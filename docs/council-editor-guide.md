# Council editor guide

Sign into https://app.pagescms.org/ with your authorized GitHub account. Open the council repository and main branch. Saving publishes automatically when Cloudflare is connected.

- **Site settings:** edit the short homepage welcome and Instagram link.
- **Project Play:** edit the short introduction and approved donation URL. Enter the confirmed goal and amount raised in CAD, then turn on Show confirmed fundraising progress. Unknown amounts stay blank; the thermometer will say totals are coming soon. Over-goal donations remain visible; only the visual fill stops at 100%. Update the date when changing totals.
- **Project Play events:** add an event title, short description, optional start/end dates, location and link. Choose Upcoming, Running or Completed, and turn on Visible. Events appear chronologically under the thermometer. Undated events appear last, ordered by the order field. Update statuses manually as events change; no automatic daily rebuild is required.

Only publish confirmed amounts, dates and links. No payment information is collected by this website. After saving, check the deployed page on your phone.
